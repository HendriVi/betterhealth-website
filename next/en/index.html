01 · Approach

Four layers, one picture.

Stress is not a character trait. It is a process in which perception, body, behaviour and environment take part at the same time. We look at them together because each one changes the others.

  1. 01

    Perception & expectation

    The brain constantly predicts what danger, effort and safety mean. These predictions shape how heavy a day feels.

    We look at Expectations · Interpretation · Attention

  2. 02

    Body & biochemistry

    Thoughts and expectations trigger bodily reactions. In turn, sleep, hormones, metabolism, inflammation and the autonomic nervous system colour what we perceive.

    We look at Sleep · Hormones · Metabolism · Nervous system

  3. 03

    Behaviour & recovery

    What we do, what we avoid and how we recover keeps a state going or releases it.

    We look at Habits · Avoidance · Recovery · Movement

  4. 04

    Environment & load

    Responsibility, workload and relationships decide how much adaptation the body has to sustain (allostatic load).

    We look at Work · Responsibility · Relationships · Rhythm

This is our understanding, not proof in an individual case. Scientific theories help us formulate questions. Clinical evidence, your circumstances and professional judgment decide which steps are justified.

03 · Process

Five steps, each with a reason.

Understand what shapes it. Identify what can be changed. Measure whether it works.

  1. 01

    Conversation

    You describe in your own words what is happening. Duration: about 90 minutes with Yolanda Vieli.

  2. 02

    Review

    We look at existing reports and results. Further investigations, where needed also by referral, happen only when they answer a specific question, with the reason and cost stated beforehand.

  3. 03

    Formulate

    You receive a written summary: what probably contributes, how sure we are, and what remains open.

  4. 04

    Plan

    We decide together on the first steps, for example conversations, behavioural work, further investigation or a referral, ordered by benefit, effort and your goals.

  5. 05

    Review again

    We check whether the expected change happens, using measures we set with you. If it does not, we reconsider the formulation and adjust the plan.

07 · Limits

Who we suit, and who we do not.

A good fit if
  • You want a thorough explanation and are ready to work on a plan.
  • Stress, anxiety, low mood or exhaustion shape your daily life.
  • You work or live in a demanding environment.
Not a fit if
  • Borderline personality disorder, schizophrenia or a schizoaffective disorder is the main issue. Other services are better suited.
  • You are in an acute crisis.
  • You mainly want purely medication-based treatment.

If we are not the right place, we will say so and help you find your way.

We do not recommend additional investigations just because they are possible or affordable. If your existing care meets your needs, you may not need our service.

Not an emergency service

In immediate danger: 144 (ambulance) or 112. If you want to talk to someone now: 143, Die Dargebotene Hand, around the clock.

08 · Questions

Questions people have before they get in touch.

Before contact
Will anyone find out that I got in touch?

Your request is read only within the practice. We give no information to third parties without your consent, unless the law requires it. You can call or write without saying anything about your symptoms. Please do not send reports or documents through the contact form.

I am asking on behalf of someone close to me. What do I do?

You are welcome to get in touch. We speak with the person concerned only with their consent. Offering a conversation without pressure often helps.

How quickly do you reply?

Usually within 24 hours, 48 hours at the latest. If it is urgent, call.

I have been told it is “just stress”.

Stress can change body and thinking in a clear way. We take that seriously and ask what exactly is happening in your case.

The first visit
What happens if you find something serious?

We tell you openly and make sure you reach the right service.

Do I need tests?

Not automatically. We only order or recommend what answers a specific question, and we tell you beforehand why and what it costs.

Can I bring documents?

Yes: reports, lab results, a medication list and earlier assessments all help.

Does this replace my doctor or therapist?

No. We complement them. With your consent we coordinate with your existing clinicians.

Fee and limits
What does it cost, and why are you a self-pay practice?

The first assessment costs CHF 1’000. We state the fee for further support in writing after the assessment. We cannot promise reimbursement by your insurer.

What do you not treat?

We do not work with borderline personality disorder, schizophrenia or schizoaffective disorders, and we are not an emergency service. Better-suited services exist; we help you find them.
